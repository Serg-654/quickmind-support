QuickMind

Capture thoughts, organize notes and tasks, and find your personal knowledge on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Your archive is saved on your device. Private iCloud sync, optional AI organization, optional Google Drive export, and invited shared folders help you use it across your tools.

Google Drive export

When you connect Google Drive, QuickMind creates a readable QuickMind Notes.txt file in your Drive and updates it after changes. It requests access only to files you use with QuickMind, rather than your entire Drive. The export can be read through a separately connected Google Drive app in ChatGPT or Codex; those services control when they retrieve or index it.
