QuickMind

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Your archive

QuickMind saves recordings, note text, tasks, cards, and history on your device. Private synchronization uses your Apple iCloud account when available. Speech recognition runs on your device. QuickMind does not upload your audio to Google Drive or an AI provider for speech recognition.

Optional AI

If you configure an external AI provider, QuickMind sends note text and relevant text context to that provider to perform the requested organization, summary, or question-answering task. The provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply. Provider credentials are stored in Keychain. You can use QuickMind without external AI.

Google account and Drive

Google Sign-In identifies the Google account you connect and stores authorization credentials in Keychain. QuickMind requests the drive.file permission, which is limited to files you use with this app, plus basic sign-in identity permissions. QuickMind creates and updates one readable snapshot named QuickMind Notes.txt in that account’s Drive. The snapshot includes current note text, tasks, dates, source links, and stable record identifiers. Audio is excluded. Deletions are reflected after the next successful export. Drive revisions and already-retrieved copies in other services are controlled by those services.

QuickMind does not scan your other Drive files, sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train generalized AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Connecting Drive to ChatGPT, Codex, or another service is a separate choice. Those services retrieve or index the exported file under their own permissions, timing, and policies. Exporting a file does not automatically update every conversation or its memory.

Shared folders

Shared folders use Apple CloudKit invitations. Only the selected shared folder and its shared notes are included. Your private archive is not automatically shared. Shared notes are not automatically sent to external AI or the personal Google Drive export. Participants’ access follows the permissions set by the owner. Offline drafts are stored locally; conflicts remain available for recovery.

Control and retention

You can turn automatic Drive export off or disconnect Google Drive in QuickMind. This stops future exports but leaves the existing file in your Drive. You can remove that file using Google Drive and revoke access in your Google account. Private deleted records can be recovered through QuickMind’s Recently Deleted feature. Shared-folder deletions apply to participants; unsent local drafts remain on the device until resolved. Apple and Google manage storage and retention within their services.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the QuickMind Google consent screen. This website itself has no analytics, advertising, forms, or cookies.
